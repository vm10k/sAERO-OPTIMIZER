How the Marginal Optimizer Protects Your APR

Standard yield boards report historical or static Stake APR. If you cast a large veNFT into a small pool with high APR, your own weight dilutes the pool immediately, causing your realized return to collapse.

  • Dynamic Dilution Awareness: Calculates Projected APR showing your true yield *after* your sAERO is allocated.
  • Micro-Chunk Slicing (100 Steps): Divides your voting power into 1% increments. Each slice is routed to the gauge producing the highest *marginal dollar gain*, optimizing real cash return.
  • Blue-Chip Liquidity Filtering: Protects you from illiquid traps by screening pools with volume thresholds (>$5,000 48H) and healthy TVL reserves.
